Broad Research Areas

My work spans natural language processing, large language models, social computing, and applied AI systems.

Sustainable AI and LLM Efficiency

IIT Kharagpur · 2024–2025

Studied the energy use of large language models across NLP tasks and investigated methods for making inference more efficient without sacrificing useful responses.

  • Benchmarked model and inference optimizations, including compression and speculative decoding.
  • Analysed the relationship between response length, user utility, and energy consumption.
  • Explored fine-tuning and prompting strategies for controlling generated output.
Related publications

Vaccine Opinions and Social Media Analytics

PhD thesis work, IIT Kharagpur · 2020–2025

Used computational methods to understand how vaccine opinions and concerns evolved on social media before and after the COVID-19 pandemic.

  • Developed the CAVES dataset for explainable multi-label classification of vaccine concerns.
  • Built Transformer and LLM-based models to identify concerns and extract supporting rationale spans.
  • Explored personalised counter-argument generation for addressing vaccine misinformation.
Related publications

Legal Document Summarization

MTech project and beyond, IIT Kharagpur · 2019–2022

Investigated extractive and abstractive summarization methods for UK and Indian Supreme Court judgments, with an emphasis on legal structure and domain knowledge.

  • Compared classical machine learning, generic, domain-specific, and Transformer-based methods.
  • Created a Linear Programming-based approach to incorporate legal-expert guidelines.
  • Controlled the balance of facts, statutes, precedents, and final judgments in summaries.
Related publications

Agentic AI, Memory, and RAG

Hewlett Packard Enterprise Labs · 2025–2026

Developed and benchmarked LLM agentic frameworks for context engineering, long-term memory, and retrieval-augmented generation.

  • Evaluated memory read/write behaviour and data management with vector and graph databases.
  • Studied conversational question answering and multi-document question answering.
  • Built evaluation harnesses for agent behaviour and latency.

Research Systems and Tools

MESSAGE CHECK

A fact-checking dashboard and predictive platform that identifies existing fact-checks matching viral misinformation claims. It supports multilingual queries using BM25+, a supervised FastText classifier, a compact semantic similarity model, and Reciprocal Rank Fusion.
